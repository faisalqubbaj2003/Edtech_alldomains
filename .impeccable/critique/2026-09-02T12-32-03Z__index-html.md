## What's Working

- **The cohort signal strip** — six 8×18px bars per student, coloured only outside that student's own baseline, grey inside. The thesis made scannable, and the one element unliftable into an unrelated SaaS.
- **Signed evidence weights**, including negative values for protective factors ("mentor session completed, −12"), making the model arguable rather than oracular.
- **"What you can and cannot see"** on the parent portal, and the teacher's "AFTER YOUR LESSON / it takes about fifteen seconds" — both start from a moment in someone's day, which is the rule every working screen follows and every generic one breaks.
- Severity is never colour-only; zero icon-only buttons missing accessible names; responsive is clean at 375/768/1440 with zero overflow; `draw()` median is 3.7ms on the heaviest 1,918-node view.
